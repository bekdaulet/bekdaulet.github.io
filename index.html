Research

My work sits at the meeting point of stellar dynamics and high-performance computing. I build and run direct N-body models on GPU clusters to follow star clusters from the moment gas is expelled until they dissolve in the Galactic tidal field.

Star cluster formation and survival

How a centrally peaked star-formation efficiency lets young clusters survive gas expulsion. Combining magnetohydrodynamics with N-body models of the earliest cluster phases.

Clusters in the Galactic tidal field

Long-term evolution of open clusters, their dissolution timescales, tidal tails, black-hole retention, and wide binary formation and disruption.

Star–disc interactions in galactic nuclei

Dynamics of nuclear star clusters around supermassive black holes, and how accretion discs shape the orbits of captured stars in active galactic nuclei.

Machine learning in astrophysics

Cluster membership from Gaia data with supervised learning, and classification of supernova gravitational-wave signals.

GPU and HPC computing

Massively parallel simulations with nbody6++gpu, phi-GRAPE, and CUDA. Benchmarking Numba, CuPy, and multi-GPU workflows for science.

Funded projects

Principal investigator of three research grants from the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of Kazakhstan on star cluster formation, dissolution, and AI-driven analysis.
